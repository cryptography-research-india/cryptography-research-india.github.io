The work involves designing and implementing the associated protocols end-to-end, so hands-on experience is essential. Ideally you've worked with MPC frameworks such as CrypTen, MP-SPDZ, or HPMPC, and FHE libraries such as Microsoft SEAL, and are comfortable going from protocol design to a working implementation. We're looking for someone with at least 2 years of experience in the field — whether from research, industry, or a strong open-source track record in secure computation. If this sounds like your kind of problem, reach out — email me at ajith.suresh@tii.ae with the subject line "Collaboration on FHE-MPC project", and let's talk about where you'd fit in.
